Team Overview

The Cincinnati Bengals are a professional football team based in Cincinnati, Ohio. They compete in the National Football League (NFL) and represent the city with their orange-and-black colors.

Team History

The Bengals were founded in 1968 by Paul Brown. Throughout their history, the team has built a passionate fan base and created many memorable moments in professional football.

The team has also made multiple Super Bowl appearances and continues to be an important part of Cincinnati's sports culture.

Fan Traditions

Game-Day Support

Fans support the Bengals by wearing team jerseys, attending games, organizing watch parties, and cheering together throughout the season.

Notable Players

Bengals fans celebrate notable players who have contributed to the team's history. These include past legends and more recent stars who have helped shape the team's identity.
